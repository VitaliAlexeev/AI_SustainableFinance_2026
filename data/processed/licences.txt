Open datasets used by AI-integrated Sustainable Finance labs:

    Climate TRACE   asset-level emissions with coordinates and owner
    World Bank      sovereign ESG panel (database 75) + country metadata
    SEC EDGAR       ticker -> CIK resolution and business addresses
    Nominatim       geocoding and reverse geocoding, cached to disk

Output layout
-------------
    data/
      raw/                       exactly as downloaded (add to .gitignore if big)
      processed/                 small, tidy, safe to commit
      MANIFEST.json              URLs, timestamps, row counts, SHA-256 digests

Licences (check before redistributing)
